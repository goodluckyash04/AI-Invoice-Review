FREIGHT SERVICES AGREEMENT
Version 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Expiry Date: December 31, 2024

================================================================================
SECTION 1 – PARTIES
================================================================================
This agreement is between Carrier Alpha Logistics ("Carrier") and any logistics
company ("Customer") that accepts these terms upon invoice submission.

================================================================================
SECTION 2 – SERVICE TYPES
================================================================================
2.1 Standard Delivery    – Transit time: 5–7 business days
2.2 Express Delivery     – Transit time: 2–3 business days
2.3 Overnight Delivery   – Next business day delivery by 10:30 AM

================================================================================
SECTION 3 – ZONE DEFINITIONS
================================================================================
Zone 1: Intra-city (< 50 km)
Zone 2: Regional (50–200 km)
Zone 3: National (200–800 km)
Zone 4: Cross-country (800–2500 km)
Zone 5: Remote / Extended area (> 2500 km)

================================================================================
SECTION 5 – SURCHARGES
================================================================================
5.1 Fuel Surcharge
    Rate: 5.5% of base freight charge
    Applied to: All shipments
    Reviewed: Monthly; rate locked for calendar month of shipment

5.2 Residential Delivery Surcharge
    Rate: INR 8.00 flat per shipment
    Applied to: Shipments delivered to residential addresses only
    Not applicable to commercial/business addresses

5.3 No other surcharges are permitted under this agreement.
    Any surcharge not listed in Sections 5.1–5.2 is not billable.

================================================================================
SECTION 6 – BILLING RULES
================================================================================
6.1 Chargeable weight is the greater of actual weight and dimensional weight.
    Dimensional weight = (L × W × H in cm) / 5000.
6.2 Weight is rounded up to the nearest 0.1 kg.
6.3 Invoices must include a valid tracking ID for each shipment line.
6.4 Duplicate invoice IDs within a billing period will be rejected.
6.5 Invoices must be submitted within 90 days of shipment date.

================================================================================
SECTION 7 – DISPUTE RESOLUTION
================================================================================
7.1 Customer may dispute any charge within 30 days of invoice date.
7.2 Disputed amounts are suspended pending investigation.
7.3 Carrier must respond within 15 business days.

================================================================================
SECTION 8 – EFFECTIVE DATE AND SUPERSESSION
================================================================================
This agreement supersedes all prior oral or written agreements between the
parties for the period January 1, 2024 through December 31, 2024.
