FREIGHT SERVICES MASTER AGREEMENT
Version 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Expiry Date: June 5, 2024

================================================================================
SECTION 1 – PARTIES
================================================================================
This agreement governs freight billing between Carrier Gamma Freight Solutions ("Carrier")
and any Customer accepting invoices under this agreement.

================================================================================
SECTION 2 – SERVICE TYPES
================================================================================
2.1 Standard Delivery    – Transit time: 4–6 business days
2.2 Express Delivery     – Transit time: 1–2 business days
2.3 Overnight Delivery   – Same/next business day delivery

================================================================================
SECTION 3 – ZONE DEFINITIONS
================================================================================
Zone 1: Metro (< 75 km)
Zone 2: Sub-regional (75–300 km)
Zone 3: National core (300–1000 km)
Zone 4: Long haul (1000–3000 km)
Zone 5: Remote (> 3000 km)

================================================================================
SECTION 5 – SURCHARGES
================================================================================
5.1 Fuel Surcharge
    Rate: 6.0% of base freight charge
    Applied to: All shipments
    Adjusted quarterly

5.2 Handling Surcharge (Special Goods)
    Rate: INR 12.00 flat per shipment
    Applied to: Shipments flagged as fragile, hazardous class D (non-dangerous),
                or oversized (longest side > 120 cm)
    Not applicable to standard goods

5.3 No other surcharges are permitted under this agreement.
    Surcharges not listed in Sections 5.1–5.2 are not billable by the Carrier.

================================================================================
SECTION 6 – BILLING RULES
================================================================================
6.1 Invoices must reference a unique tracking ID per shipment.
6.2 Weight billed must match actual measured weight within ±0.5 kg tolerance.
6.3 Invoices submitted more than 60 days after delivery date are void.
6.4 Duplicate invoice numbers within a billing cycle are automatically rejected.

================================================================================
SECTION 7 – DISPUTE AND AUDIT RIGHTS
================================================================================
7.1 Customer may request weight-audit certificates within 45 days of invoice.
7.2 Rate disputes must be raised within 30 days with supporting shipment data.

================================================================================
SECTION 8 – AGREEMENT TERM
================================================================================
Effective January 1, 2024 through December 31, 2024.
Supersedes all previous Carrier Beta rate schedules.
