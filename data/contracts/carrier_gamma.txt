CARRIER GAMMA FREIGHT SOLUTIONS
MASTER RATE AGREEMENT
Version 2.0 – MID-YEAR RATE REVISION
Effective Date: June 1, 2024
Expiry Date: December 31, 2024
(Supersedes Version 1.0 for the period June 1 – December 31, 2024)

================================================================================
NOTICE OF OVERLAPPING VERSIONS
================================================================================
Version 1.0 of this agreement (effective Jan 1 – May 31, 2024) remains on file.
For shipments between January 1 and May 31, 2024, Version 1.0 rates apply.
For shipments from June 1, 2024 onward, Version 2.0 rates in this document apply.
Human review is required for any shipment dated May 31 – June 1 (boundary cases).

================================================================================
SECTION 1 – SERVICE TYPES
================================================================================
1.1 Standard Delivery    – Transit time: 5–8 business days
1.2 Express Delivery     – Transit time: 2–3 business days
1.3 Overnight Delivery   – Next business day by noon

================================================================================
SECTION 2 – ZONE DEFINITIONS
================================================================================
Zone 1: Local (< 100 km)
Zone 2: Regional (100–400 km)
Zone 3: National (400–1200 km)
Zone 4: Long haul (1200–2800 km)
Zone 5: Extreme remote (> 2800 km)

================================================================================
SECTION 4 – SURCHARGES (Version 2.0)
================================================================================
4.1 Fuel Surcharge
    Rate: 7.0% of base freight charge (increased from 6.5% in v1)
    Applied to: All shipments from June 1, 2024

4.2 Residential Delivery Surcharge
    Rate: INR 9.50 flat per shipment (increased from INR 8.00 in v1)
    Applied to: Residential addresses only

4.3 No other surcharges are billable under Version 2.0.

================================================================================
SECTION 5 – BILLING RULES
================================================================================
5.1 Valid tracking ID required on every invoice line.
5.2 Weight rounded to nearest 0.1 kg; disputes within ±0.1 kg waived.
5.3 Submit invoices within 75 days of ship date.

================================================================================
SECTION 6 – VERSION CONFLICT RESOLUTION
================================================================================
6.1 If a shipment date falls within the effective period of both v1 and v2,
    the invoice is flagged for human review.
6.2 Carrier may not charge v2 rates for shipments made before June 1, 2024.
